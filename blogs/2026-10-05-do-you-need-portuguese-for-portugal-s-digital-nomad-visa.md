Short answer : No. Portugal's digital nomad visa, the D8, has no language requirement, and your Portuguese is never tested at any stage of the application. What it does require is €3,680 a month in income earned outside Portugal, a Portuguese tax number, a twelve-month lease and a clean criminal record.
