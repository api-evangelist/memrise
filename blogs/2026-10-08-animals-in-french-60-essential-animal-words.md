Spider TL;DR : French animal vocabulary includes familiar words such as “le chien” (dog) and “le chat” (cat) for household pets. Some of the animal names are very similar to the English words. This can be helpful for learning, but every animal noun has a grammatical gender, so beginners should learn each word with un, une, le, or la.
