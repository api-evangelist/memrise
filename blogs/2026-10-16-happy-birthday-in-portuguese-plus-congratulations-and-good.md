TL;DR: Happy birthday in Portuguese is feliz aniversário; you can also add parabéns, which means congratulations, or boa sorte, which means good luck. These phrases are used in both Brazilian and European Portuguese, though pronunciation and some informal expressions may vary between the two varieties. This makes them useful phrases to learn together, whether you are visiting Portugal or Brazil.
