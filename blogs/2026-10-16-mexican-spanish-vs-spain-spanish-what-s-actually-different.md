TL;DR: While Mexican Spanish and Spain Spanish are varieties of the same language, they differ in pronunciation, vocabulary, and some grammar. The clearest example is the plural form of you, which is vosotros in Spain and ustedes in Mexican Spanish. Vocabulary also varies, with words such as a computer translated as computadora in Mexico and ordenador in Spain.
