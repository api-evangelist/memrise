TL;DR: The most common French family words include père (father), mère (mother), frère (brother), sœur (sister), fils (son), fille (daughter), mari (husband), femme (wife), oncle (uncle), and tante (aunt). French, just like English, also distinguishes between singular and plural forms and between masculine and feminine relatives. Learners usually encounter family vocabulary during the first stages of learning French.
