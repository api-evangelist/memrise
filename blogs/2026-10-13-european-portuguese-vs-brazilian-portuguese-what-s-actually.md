TL;DR : European Portuguese and Brazilian Portuguese are varieties of the same language, and speakers can generally understand each other, but they differ in vocabulary, pronunciation, and grammar. The European variety reduces some unstressed vowels, and it is the ideal choice if you plan to live in Portugal. The Brazilian variety is common in Brazil, making it helpful for learners who plan to visit the region.
