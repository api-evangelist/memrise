TL;DR: When learning Mexican Spanish, individuals should start with everyday vocabulary, pronunciation, essential grammar, and phrases used in Mexico. This provides a solid foundation for building listening and speaking around real conversation. Beginners can start with greetings, numbers, introductions, food, and directions before moving into regional expressions.
